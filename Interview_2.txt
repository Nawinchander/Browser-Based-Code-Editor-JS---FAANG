Interview Questions — Incremental Rendering
Q1. Why not render all lines?
Answer

Large DOM trees:

Increase memory usage
Slow layout calculations
Cause FPS drops

Virtualization reduces DOM nodes drastically.

Q2. Complexity Improvement?

Without virtualization:

O(n)

With viewport rendering:

O(v)

Interview Questions — IndexedDB
Q1. Why use IndexedDB instead of LocalStorage?
Answer

IndexedDB supports:

Async operations
Large storage
Transactions
Structured objects

LocalStorage is synchronous and blocks UI.

Q2. Why separate persistence service from DB service?
Answer

DB service handles low-level storage.

Persistence service handles:

Business rules
Syncing
Autosave orchestration
Conflict handling
